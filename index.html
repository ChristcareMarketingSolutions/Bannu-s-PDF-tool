    Free, private PDF tools — no uploads, no sign-up

    Bannu’s PDF Tool is a free online PDF editor that works completely on your own device. Unlike most PDF websites, your documents are never uploaded to a server, which makes it safe for contracts, invoices, drawings, reports and other confidential files.

    Organise pages

    • Merge PDF – combine multiple PDFs into one file
    • Split PDF – split by page ranges or every N pages
    • Extract pages – save selected pages as a new PDF
    • Delete pages – remove unwanted pages
    • Reorder pages – drag and drop to rearrange
    • Rotate PDF – rotate single or all pages

    Convert & optimise

    • PDF to JPG / PNG – up to 300 DPI
    • JPG to PDF – images to A4, Letter or custom pages
    • Compress PDF – reduce PDF file size
    • Watermark PDF – add text watermarks
    • Add page numbers to any PDF

    Security

    • Protect PDF – add a password
    • Unlock PDF – remove a password you know

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. Every tool runs inside your web browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device, so the tool is safe for confidential documents.

    Is Bannu’s PDF Tool free?

    Yes. All tools are completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark on your output and no file-count limits.

    Does it work on mobile phones?

    Yes. The site works in modern browsers on Android, iPhone, tablets, Windows, macOS and Linux.

    How do I merge PDF files?

    Open the Merge PDF tool, add two or more PDF files, drag them into the order you want, then click Merge to download a single combined PDF.

    Can I reduce the size of a PDF?

    Yes. The Compress PDF tool offers lossless clean-up, image optimisation and maximum compression levels. If the result is not smaller, your original file is kept.
